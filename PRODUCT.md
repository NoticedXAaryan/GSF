# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Students, aspiring founders, entrepreneurs, and startup enablers (investors, mentors). Their context is looking to gain actionable insights, build their network, pitch their ideas, and secure a schedule at a high-energy startup festival. The job to be done is to get inspired, register for masterclasses, and explore the event schedule.

## Product Purpose
A premier two-day entrepreneurship festival serving as a launchpad bridging academia, industry, and the startup ecosystem. Its purpose is to drive engagement, registrations for masterclasses, and participation in pitching competitions. Success means a seamless, exciting user journey from landing page to digital pass creation.

## Brand Personality
Bold, dynamic, ambitious, high-energy, and premium. It should feel like a serious, top-tier tech/startup conference (like a Vercel or Stripe event) but with the vibrant energy of a university ecosystem. 

## Anti-references
Generic academic portals, card-heavy standard admin dashboards, boring standard bootstrap layouts. Standard "AI-generated" tropes: no side-stripe borders, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, no identical card grids repeated endlessly.

## Design Principles
- Show, don't tell: Use dynamic layouts and purposeful motion.
- Premium restraint: A focused color strategy that isn't a generic dark mode.
- Clear typography hierarchy over excessive decoration.
- Impeccable craftsmanship: Every hover, focus, and state must feel intentional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast minimum. Visible focus states for all interactive elements. Semantic HTML structure for screen readers. Respect for prefers-reduced-motion.
